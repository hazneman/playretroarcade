How to Play

Aim with the mouse — the target moves inside the goal.
Hold the mouse button to charge power, release to shoot.

More power beats the keeper's dive, but the shot scatters more — and corners score bonus points. Three saves or misses end the game.

The keeper learns every level. Good luck. 🧤

Scoring

Goal: 100 + level bonus
Corner shots: up to +60
Level up every 5 goals

⚽ Penalty Shootout

You against the keeper. Pick your corner, charge the shot, beat the dive. Three failed attempts and the whistle blows.
