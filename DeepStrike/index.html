How to Play

You are the yellow submarine. 🟡

Arrows / WASD — dive, climb and cruise underwater.
Space — fire a torpedo. It swims up, and past the surface it becomes a missile — ships and planes are fair game.

Planes drop depth charges that sink and explode. Keep your distance or outrun the blast. Three hits and the hull gives in.

Scoring

Ship: 100 (+speed bonus)
Plane: 250
Level up every 1000 points

🤿 Deep Strike

Enemy convoys above, patrol planes in the sky, one yellow submarine below. Torpedo the ships, snipe the planes, dodge the depth charges.
